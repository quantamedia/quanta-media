Media and Entertainment :
The way we make people explore their ideas

Quanta Media supports and connects the thoughts and ideas of people.
We produce short films, Documentaries, Educational content, Digital and Hard
copy publication and other media related initiatives to entertain people through the field of media.

Our student team produces short films and documentaries through vijay
studios. We launch educational content through various social media
platforms and publish various books, articles, poems and magazines
through our trusted publishers.

Our team also invite people with a good script, screenplay, concept or a skill to produce
creative short films and educational documentaries and in return we support and value
them with a recognition in our quanta productions.
