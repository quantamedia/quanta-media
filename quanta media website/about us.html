Who we are - An Introduction about a student project

Quanta Media is a creative students startup initiative founded by Dharshan K and his team to benefit the people in the field of education, media and entertaiment to support the social welfare of them. In the growing age of AI and technology we develop the oppurtunities and services to benefit the overall expectations from a brand like us.

what does quanta media do

Quanta media offers various services and products to people in the field of education, media and documentation. We solve problems, collect data and entertain people through a small but dedicated team of students. Users can register for our projects, and other initiatives which gives users a support. We collect data from our users to solve problems and in return we value them with recognition and a chance to be in our production team. We create software and hardware applications to solve problems, produce films in our quanta productions. Quanta media runs now in krishnagiri district and it will soon expand all over tamil nadu with the support of our users.
